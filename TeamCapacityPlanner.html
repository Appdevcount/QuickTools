💡 How capacity is calculated

  • Working days = business days between start & end dates (Mon–Fri only)
  • Available hours = (Working Days − Leave Days) × Hours/Day × Availability %
  • Net hours = Available Hours × (1 − Buffer %) — time for actual dev work
